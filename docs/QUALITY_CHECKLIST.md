# Shot and Film QC Checklist

## Character continuity
- Correct character identity/reference and age range.
- Face, hair, body shape, costume and accessories remain consistent within the scene.
- No accidental extra people or duplicated body parts.
- Expression and body language fit the scripted beat.

## Scene continuity
- Location geometry, time of day, lighting direction and props are consistent.
- Screen direction and eyelines are plausible across cuts.
- The shot does not reveal information before the script allows it.
- No unrequested text, logos, watermark-like artifacts or unintended readable signage.

## Motion and image
- Motion begins and ends cleanly, with enough handles for editing.
- No severe warping, flicker, changing faces, broken hands, sliding objects or camera teleportation.
- Focus, composition and movement support the story beat.
- Frame dimensions and aspect ratio match the project's delivery profile.

## Dialogue and sound
- Correct line assigned to the intended character.
- Pronunciation, pacing and emotional direction reviewed.
- Dialogue is intelligible over music and ambience.
- No clipping, abrupt cuts, unexpected silence or unsafely high peaks.
- Voice and audio asset permissions are documented.

## Final delivery
- Every timeline media path exists.
- Runtime and export dimensions match settings.
- No missing frames, black gaps or unintended frozen frames.
- Subtitles and titles are spelled correctly and timed appropriately.
- Credits and asset/model-license manifest are included where needed.
- Final file plays fully in at least one independent player.
